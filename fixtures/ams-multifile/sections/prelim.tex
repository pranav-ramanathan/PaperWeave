\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:prelim}

Throughout, $\Seq$ denotes the set of finite sequences over a fixed alphabet,
and $\len(s)$ is the length of $s \in \Seq$.

\begin{definition}\label{def:splice}
A \emph{splice} is a triple $(i, d, t)$ with $0 \le i \le \len(s)$, a deletion
count $d \ge 0$ and inserted text $t \in \Seq$.
\end{definition}

We follow the notation of \textcite{shapiro2011}; see also \cite[Section~2]{shapiro2011}.
