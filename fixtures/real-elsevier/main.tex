\begin{abstract}
River gauges are read far more often than the water changes. We pick the
readings that matter and show that a tenth of them recover the same
flow curve.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Gauges report every minute, yet daily flow moves slowly \cite{lindqvist19}.
We ask which readings can be dropped.

\section{Method}
Let $q(t)$ be the flow. We keep a reading when
\begin{equation}
  |q(t)-q(t_0)| > \epsilon .
\end{equation}

\section{Results}
Keeping a tenth of the readings gives the same curve to within noise.
