\begin{abstract}
We count cycles that never share an edge with a longer cycle. A short
argument bounds their number and a small program confirms the bound.
\keywords{graphs \and cycles \and counting}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Cycles in sparse graphs are well studied \cite{brandt18}. Quiet cycles are
a new kind and we bound them here.

\section{Bound}
\begin{claim}
A graph on $n$ vertices has at most $n$ quiet cycles.
\end{claim}
\begin{proof}
Charge each quiet cycle to its smallest vertex. No vertex is charged twice.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}
Every quiet cycle has a vertex of degree two.
\end{lemma}
